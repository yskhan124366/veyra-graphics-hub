Services

Veyra Graphics Hub provides brand identity, packaging, editorial and campaign design. Every project is quoted individually, based on a written brief.

Quotes and payment

  • A 50% deposit is due before work starts, and the remaining 50% on delivery.
  • Final files are released once the project is paid in full.
  • Payment is by bank transfer or through Upwork. Projects contracted on Upwork follow Upwork's payment and milestone system.
  • Prices are in US dollars (USD) unless agreed otherwise in writing.

Project scope and revisions

  • The scope of each project is defined in a written brief that we agree before work starts.
  • Revisions beyond the agreed scope are quoted separately before any extra work begins.
  • Unused revision rounds do not carry forward to later projects.

Intellectual property

  • You own the final approved deliverables once the project is paid in full.
  • We may show the work in our portfolio and marketing, unless we have signed a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) with you.
  • Editable source files (for example AI or PSD) are available at an additional cost.

Client responsibilities

You agree to provide a clear brief, any brand assets we need (logos, fonts, photos, dielines), and timely feedback. Delays in receiving these extend the project timeline accordingly.

Limitation of liability

Our total liability for any project is limited to the fee paid for that project. We are not responsible for printing or manufacturing errors made by third parties when the files we supplied match the specifications you provided.

Cancellation

If a project is cancelled part-way through, the deposit is refunded in proportion to the work that has not yet been delivered.
