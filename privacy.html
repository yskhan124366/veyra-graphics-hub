Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This is a starter policy reviewed for plain-language clarity. For full legal compliance, have a lawyer review it before publishing.

Who we are

Veyra Graphics Hub is an independent design agency founded by Younas Khan. We design brand identity, packaging, editorial and campaign work for clients worldwide. You can reach us at ys.khan124366@gmail.com.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service run by GitHub, Inc.

What we collect

  • Inquiry form: when you use the form on our website, we receive your name, email address, project type, budget range and message. The form is processed by Formspree.
  • Messages you send us: anything you include when you contact us by email, WhatsApp, Upwork or LinkedIn.
  • Server logs: GitHub Pages keeps standard technical logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, to run and secure the service.
  • Cookies: we do not currently set any cookies, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To plan, deliver and invoice the work you hire us for.
  • We do not send marketing emails without your consent.
  • We never sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We only share information with the services we need to run this website and our work:

  • Formspree receives inquiry form submissions and forwards them to us.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Google (Gmail) is our email provider. Our website fonts are also loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google can see your IP address when the page loads.
  • WhatsApp, Upwork and LinkedIn handle any messages you choose to send us there, under their own privacy policies.

We do not share your information with advertisers.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • receive a copy of your information in a portable format.

To make a request, email ys.khan124366@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. You will not be treated differently for exercising any of these rights.

Data retention

  • Inquiries are kept for up to 24 months, unless you become a client.
  • Project files and related messages for clients are kept for 5 years.

After these periods, we delete the information or keep it only in a form that no longer identifies you.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Email ys.khan124366@gmail.com.
