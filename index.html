Malaika Perfume

Malaika needed retail packaging that would make a round branded sticker feel like a premium fragrance gift. We designed a four-part nested system: a window carton, a chair insert, a 3 × 3 in product box and the sticker itself, all built on the client's original dielines.

Common questions

Before You Get in Touch

Quick answers to what most clients ask first.

How long does a brand identity project take?

Most brand identity projects take 3–5 weeks from the agreed brief to final files, depending on scope and how quickly feedback comes back.

How long does packaging design take?

Typically 2–3 weeks for a label, and 4–6 weeks for a full structural packaging system with dielines and inserts.

What's included in a project price?

Every project includes a written brief, initial concepts, the agreed rounds of revisions and final files in the formats you need. Brand identity projects also include a short set of usage guidelines.

How many rounds of revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions are standard. Additional rounds are quoted separately before any extra work starts.

Do you work with US/UK clients?

Yes. We work remote-first with clients worldwide. We're based in GMT+3, which overlaps with UK working hours and the US East Coast morning, and we reply within 1–2 business days.

Who owns the final files?

You own the approved final deliverables once the project is paid in full. Editable source files (AI/PSD) are available at an additional cost.

Do you coordinate printing or manufacturing?

Yes. We supply print-ready files and dielines, and we can liaise directly with your printer or manufacturer.

What's your typical starting budget?

Brand identity starts from $1,800, packaging from $1,200, editorial design from $900 and campaign artwork from $600. Every project is quoted once we've seen the brief.
